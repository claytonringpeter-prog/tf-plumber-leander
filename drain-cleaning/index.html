Drain Cleaning in Leander, TX

We clear slow, gurgling, blocked, and backed-up drains across Leander.

Call (512) 799-9083 Ask a question

The sink gurgles, the shower holds water, or a sewer smell comes up from the drain. We trace the blockage from the fixture opening toward the branch line or cleanout.

We clear buildup from kitchen sinks, tubs, showers, floor drains, and laundry drains. Hair, soap residue, grease, food waste, and paper products can narrow a pipe until water has nowhere to go.

We recommend prompt work if water is backing up, a floor drain is filling, or more than one fixture is slow. A drain blockage can move through connected pipes and force wastewater out at a lower fixture.

Gurgling drains and sewer smell

We hear reports of a kitchen sink that drains slowly, a tub that fills around the ankles, and a toilet that bubbles when the washing machine drains. We also check drains with a sewer smell, black residue around the opening, or water returning after the faucet is off.

We look at each fixture because a clog near a sink trap behaves differently from a blockage farther down a shared line. Similar sounds and standing water can come from separate faults, and the work changes once we locate the pipe section involved.

Drain camera inspection

We start the callout by testing the affected fixture and nearby drains. We check water flow, listen for gurgling, inspect the trap and drain opening, and locate any accessible cleanout.

We may run a cable through the line to break up or retrieve the blockage. A straightforward fixture drain check and clearing often takes around twenty to forty minutes, depending on the route and material in the pipe.

We use a drain camera when the blockage returns, the cable meets a hard obstruction, or several fixtures share the problem. We can usually clear accessible buildup during the visit, while damaged pipe, root intrusion, or a buried line may need separate scheduling and further repair work.

Pipe access and blockage depth

We base the scope on pipe access, blockage depth, and pipe material. A clog at a visible sink trap is different from buildup beyond a cleanout or inside an older cast-iron branch line.

We also account for the route to the drain opening, the condition of the cleanout cap, and whether cabinets, panels, or flooring block access. A seized cleanout or a line that needs camera inspection adds work before we can reach the obstruction.

We explain if the blockage appears limited to one fixture or extends into a shared drain line. We also flag situations where a pipe section, cleanout fitting, or outside line needs additional work after the clearing attempt.

Fixture location and cleanout access

Tell us which room has the slow or backed-up fixture, such as the kitchen sink, hall bath tub, or laundry drain. Let us know whether one drain is affected or water rises in another fixture.

We can also use the location of any cleanout cap, even if it is in a garage, yard, closet, or crawlspace. Please mention standing water, sewer smell, gurgling, or a drain that is running back now.

Clear items from under the sink or around the tub access panel if you can do so safely. Call TF Plumber Leander at (512) 799-9083 before a blocked line pushes wastewater into another fixture or across the floor.
